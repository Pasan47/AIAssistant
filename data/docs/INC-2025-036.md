# INC-2025-036 - notification-service (SEV3)

## Summary
Date: 2025-10-01. Service: notification-service. Severity: SEV3. Duration: 20 minutes. Root cause: email provider rate limiting.

## What happened
SMS/email payment confirmations were delayed because the email provider throttled our burst sending. Customer-facing payment failures were observed during the window.

## Resolution
Sending was batched and rate-limited.

## Follow-up actions
Move to queue-based sending with backoff.

## Lessons learned
Detection came from customer-impact alerts rather than leading indicators; see the incident severity policy POL-002.
