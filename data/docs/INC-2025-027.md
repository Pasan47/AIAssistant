# INC-2025-027 - card-authorization (SEV1)

## Summary
Date: 2025-08-02. Service: card-authorization. Severity: SEV1. Duration: 77 minutes. Root cause: third-party processor timeout.

## What happened
AcquirerX suffered a regional outage; authorisations timed out and our failover did not trigger because it was still manual. Customer-facing payment failures were observed during the window.

## Resolution
Engineers manually enabled the secondary processor route.

## Follow-up actions
Automate processor failover (carry-over action from INC-2025-009).

## Lessons learned
Detection came from customer-impact alerts rather than leading indicators; see the incident severity policy POL-002.
