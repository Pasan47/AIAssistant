# INC-2025-009 - card-authorization (SEV2)

## Summary
Date: 2025-03-27. Service: card-authorization. Severity: SEV2. Duration: 42 minutes. Root cause: third-party processor timeout.

## What happened
The external card processor (AcquirerX) responded in over 12s for 18% of authorisations; our 10s client timeout failed them as declines. Customer-facing payment failures were observed during the window.

## Resolution
Traffic was shifted to the secondary processor via the routing flag payments.processor.route.

## Follow-up actions
Introduce circuit breaker with automatic failover; negotiate processor latency SLA.

## Lessons learned
Detection came from customer-impact alerts rather than leading indicators; see the incident severity policy POL-002.
