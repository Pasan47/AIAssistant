# INC-2025-021 - payments-api (SEV2)

## Summary
Date: 2025-06-19. Service: payments-api. Severity: SEV2. Duration: 55 minutes. Root cause: expired TLS certificate.

## What happened
The mutual-TLS certificate to the payments partner network expired; all partner payment calls failed handshake. Customer-facing payment failures were observed during the window.

## Resolution
A renewed certificate was deployed from the secrets vault.

## Follow-up actions
Automate renewal and alert 30 days before expiry.

## Lessons learned
Detection came from customer-impact alerts rather than leading indicators; see the incident severity policy POL-002.
