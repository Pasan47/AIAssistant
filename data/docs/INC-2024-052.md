# INC-2024-052 - payment-gateway (SEV2)

## Summary
Date: 2024-10-22. Service: payment-gateway. Severity: SEV2. Duration: 63 minutes. Root cause: message queue backlog.

## What happened
A consumer bug stalled the payment-events queue, delaying settlement notifications. Customer-facing payment failures were observed during the window.

## Resolution
Consumer was patched and backlog replayed.

## Follow-up actions
Add queue-depth alerting.

## Lessons learned
Detection came from customer-impact alerts rather than leading indicators; see the incident severity policy POL-002.
