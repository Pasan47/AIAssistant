# INC-2025-014 - payment-gateway (SEV1)

## Summary
Date: 2025-05-06. Service: payment-gateway. Severity: SEV1. Duration: 128 minutes. Root cause: database connection pool exhaustion.

## What happened
A long-running reconciliation query held connections open during peak hour. Pool exhaustion caused 31% of payment attempts to fail; an estimated 14,200 customers were affected. Customer-facing payment failures were observed during the window.

## Resolution
Reconciliation job was killed, connections were recycled, and the job was moved to a read replica.

## Follow-up actions
Enforce statement timeouts (30s) and separate pools for batch and online workloads.

## Lessons learned
Detection came from customer-impact alerts rather than leading indicators; see the incident severity policy POL-002.
