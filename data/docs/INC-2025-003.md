# INC-2025-003 - payment-gateway (SEV1)

## Summary
Date: 2025-02-11. Service: payment-gateway. Severity: SEV1. Duration: 95 minutes. Root cause: database connection pool exhaustion.

## What happened
A promotional campaign tripled card-payment traffic. The gateway's PostgreSQL pool (max 50) saturated and requests queued until clients timed out. Customer-facing payment failures were observed during the window.

## Resolution
On-call raised pool size to 150 and restarted two gateway pods; traffic drained within 20 minutes.

## Follow-up actions
Add pool saturation alerting at 70% and load-test before campaigns.

## Lessons learned
Detection came from customer-impact alerts rather than leading indicators; see the incident severity policy POL-002.
